S3 SIGN TRIMCAP LED LETTER
ARUMBAKKAM, CHENNAI

Trim-Cap LED 3D Letters are a stylish and high-impact signage option designed to give brands a premium and eye-catching look. These letters feature a glossy acrylic front with a colored trim cap around the edges, creating clear borders and a neat finish that enhances the overall design. The sidewalls are usually made of durable materials like aluminum or ABS, ensuring the letters stay strong and weather-resistant for outdoor use. Inside each letter, bright LED modules are installed to provide uniform glow and maximum visibility during both day and night. Trim-Cap letters can be customized in any font, size, or color to match a brand’s identity perfectly. They are energy-efficient, low-maintenance, and long-lasting, making them a popular choice for retail shops, restaurants, malls, and corporate businesses. With their modern appearance and professional illumination, these letters help attract customers and strengthen brand presence effectively.

S3 SIGN BACKLIGHT BOARD
ARUMBAKKAM CHENNAI

Backlight Boards are a highly attractive illuminated signage solution that helps a business stand out with bright visibility and a clean modern look. These boards are designed with a high-quality printed display surface on the front, supported by an internal LED lighting system that spreads light evenly from behind. This glowing back illumination highlights the graphics, making the board easy to read from long distances, especially at night or in low-light areas. The structure is built using durable materials such as aluminum frames and acrylic panels to ensure long-lasting outdoor performance. Backlight Boards offer full customization in size, layout, color, and branding style, making them ideal for shops, restaurants, showrooms, theaters, fuel stations, and commercial buildings looking to attract more customers. They are energy-efficient, low-maintenance, and deliver a premium appearance that enhances brand identity and improves visibility 24/7.

S3 SIGN METAL LETTER
ARUMBAKKAM CHENNAI

Metal Letters with Light provide a premium and stylish signage look that instantly elevates a brand’s identity. Made from strong metals like stainless steel or aluminum, these letters offer elegant finishes such as brushed, mirror, or titanium. LED lighting is installed inside or behind the letters to create a bright and attractive glow that makes the signage stand out day and night. Perfect for hotels, showrooms, boutiques, and corporate offices, these illuminated metal letters are weather resistant, durable, and energy efficient. They deliver long-lasting performance and give any business a classy, modern appearance that attracts customers easily.

S3 SIGN CNC Cutting LED BOARD
ARUMBAKKAM CHENNAI

CNC Cutting LED Boards are modern illuminated signboards created using precision CNC cutting technology for clean shapes, perfect edges, and highly detailed design results. These boards feature acrylic or metal panels cut with exact accuracy, combined with high-brightness LEDs installed behind or inside the design. The lighting enhances the cutout areas, creating an attractive glow that improves visibility day and night. CNC LED boards are fully customizable in size, logo shapes, patterns, and fonts, making them ideal for retail shops, restaurants, showrooms, and commercial branding. They are durable, energy efficient, and give any business a premium, eye-catching identity.

S3 SIGN LOLLIPOP BOARD
ARUMBAKKAM CHENNAI

Lollipop Boards are double-sided circular signboards mounted on a vertical pole, designed to attract attention from both directions. They are commonly placed near shop entrances or sidewalks to improve visibility for passing customers. The board can be illuminated using internal LEDs or used as a non-lit display depending on the branding requirement. Made with durable materials like acrylic, metal, or flex, lollipop boards are fully customizable in size, shape, color, and printing style. They are ideal for small shops, restaurants, bakeries, salons, pharmacies, and street-facing businesses that want a clear and attractive identity visible even from a distance.
